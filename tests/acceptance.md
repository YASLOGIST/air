# Air acceptance checklist

- App loads with the navigation, hero, calculator, tracker, and footer visible.
- Theme and language preferences survive a reload.
- AWB modal opens from the primary navigation and closes with Escape.
- Legal modal traps focus and returns focus to its trigger.
- Calculator updates chargeable weight and carbon estimates when dimensions change.
- ULD viewer remains usable with keyboard controls and reduced-motion enabled.
- Skip link moves focus to the main content.
